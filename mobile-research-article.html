By TechNova Research

Introduction

Smartphones combine computing, communication and multimedia technologies in a portable device. They allow users to communicate, access information and use a wide range of digital services.

Key Smartphone Technologies

Modern smartphones use processors to run applications, displays to present information, cameras to capture images, and wireless technologies to connect to networks.
